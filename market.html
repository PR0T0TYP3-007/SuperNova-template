Market run questions

Who does the shopping?

Our partner vendors. They buy your list, pack it, and hand it to our rider.

How much does it cost?

Whatever the market charges for your items, plus a flat service fee and delivery. You see all of it in the quote before you pay a kobo.

What if something isn't available?

We'll tell you in the quote and suggest a swap. You can accept it, drop it, or change the list.

Can I set a budget?

Yes. Add one to your list and we'll keep the quote under it, or tell you what to cut.
