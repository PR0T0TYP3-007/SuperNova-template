Flight questions

How fast do I get options?

Usually within a few hours. Last-minute trips get bumped to the front of the queue.

Do I pay when I send the request?

No. You only pay once you've picked a flight from your shortlist.

How is it cheaper than booking myself?

Our travel partners have access to fares that aren't on the public booking sites. We pass those savings straight to you.

How does the concierge pick-up work?

Tick the box when you request. Once you book, we arrange a driver to collect you from campus in good time for your flight.
