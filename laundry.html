Laundry questions

When do I pay?

After pickup. We count your items, confirm the price, and send you a payment link. No surprises.

How long does it take?

About 48 hours. Express gets it back the next day for a small extra fee.

Can you do native wear and agbada?

Yes, and carefully. They go to our partner's dry clean line, not the regular wash.

What if something gets damaged?

Report it from your order within 24 hours of delivery and we'll sort it out with the cleaner.
