Good questions

Before you order

Who runs Supernova?

Supernova is an independent student platform built by 3Projects. Your food is cooked and handled by Crunchies and Roots, the kitchens you already know.

Can I mix Crunchies and Roots in one order?

Not in one bag. Each kitchen has its own bag and its own checkout, so your food is never held up waiting for the other kitchen. Fill both bags if you like and check each one out.

Is it safe to pay?

Your card details go straight to our payment provider's secure page and are never seen or stored by Supernova. An order is only confirmed once the payment provider confirms it.

Something was missing. What now?

Open the order and tap "Report a problem". Choose missing item, wrong order or late order, and we'll review it. Refunds go back to the way you paid.

When are flights coming?

Soon. We're setting it up with a licensed travel partner first. Join the flights list above and you'll hear the day it opens.
