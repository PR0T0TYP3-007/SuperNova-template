Good questions

You asked, we answered

Who runs Supernova?

Supernova is an independent student platform built by 3Projects. Food comes from Crunchies and Roots, market items from our partner vendors, and dry cleaning from our partner cleaners.

Will you deliver to my room?

If your school lets our riders in, we'll come to your hostel. If security stops them at the gate, we'll call you to come out and collect. Rules are rules, abeg.

Can I mix Crunchies and Roots in one order?

Not in one bag. Each kitchen has its own bag and checkout, so your food never waits on the other kitchen. Fill both if you're that hungry.

How do market runs work?

Send your list, our partners send a quote, you pay if you're happy, and we deliver. Nothing is charged until you say yes to the price.

When do I pay for dry cleaning?

After pickup. We count your clothes, confirm the price, then send you a payment link.

Is it safe to pay?

Yes. Card details go straight to our payment partner's secure page. We never see or store them.

Something went wrong. Now what?

Open the order and tap "Report a problem". We'll look into it, and if we got it wrong, you get refunded the way you paid.

How do flights work?

Tell us where you're flying from and to, your dates, and any airline you prefer. Within a few hours we send a shortlist of deals. Pick one, pay, done. We can also arrange a concierge pick-up to the airport.
